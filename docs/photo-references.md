## Observations from actual viewed photographs

- `2024-08-28_041.JPG` and `_042.JPG`: SHIBUYA109's cylindrical front tower is visible at the far left. Light silver square cladding grid wraps around the curve; contemporary magenta 109 logo near the top; thin dark horizontal seam beneath the logo band; a tall campaign panel occupies the middle. A significantly higher, narrow rectangular tower with blue vertical glazing is behind it in the image, but this image alone does not establish whether that tower is structurally part of 109. The base/entrance is obscured by trees, vehicles, and distance. These are two nearby street positions, not a complete multi-view survey. They cannot justify accurately modeled hidden elevations or measured dimensions.
- `2024-08-28_038.JPG`: strong QFRONT/Starbucks reference. Convex broad glass frontage, a dense grid of thin vertical mullions, large screen above a two-story transparent retail base, multiple STARBUCKS wordmarks, white diagonal interior braces, and a rooftop billboard with visible light-arm supports. Upper mass has a stepped shoulder on the right rather than one simple box.
- `2024-10-11_005.JPG`: MAGNET by SHIBUYA109 (distinct from main 109). Long pale slab facade with narrow black horizontal ribbon windows; dark glass upper setback; rounded vertical corner campaign board; large ground-level digital sign; black coffee shop frontage in the right corner.
- `2024-10-11_043.JPG`: Center-gai shops. Narrow plots, different floor heights and sign sizes, projecting shallow metal awnings, lit interiors behind real shop openings, vertical tenant sign stacks, vending machines clustered along the wall, textured paving and four-globe streetlights. This informs reusable shop modules and street props.

### Concrete changes justified by these close-ups

- The front cylinder is a slender vertical element; its visible height is approximately 4–5 times its width, not the much fatter cylinder in the first distant-photo draft. The logo crown height is roughly 0.6 of the cylinder diameter. The study retains a compressed height around 47.4 m and reduces the cylinder diameter to about 10.6 m. These are interpreted proportions, not measured dimensions.
- The lower wings are predominantly opaque silver paneled walls with deep, full-height dark glazed recesses next to the cylinder. Their upper fascia projects across the recess. The earlier rows of invented office windows were removed.
- A freestanding-looking glazed lift enclosure with a close mullion grid appears on the right; a fine exposed structural lattice carrying a large secondary poster occupies the left recess.
- The cylinder has narrow, horizontal recessed slots at repeated levels. In the 2020 aerial, three are exposed below the central campaign and others are covered by it. The model includes actual breaks in the curved skin, recessed dark panels, upper lips and mullions.
- The main entrance is a relatively small rectangular opening. A gold-colored open steel truss canopy projects over the pavement, supported by slender columns. Adjacent side-entrance terrace and right-hand external steps are visible and now modeled.
- The crown is open at the top, with a dark internal void, perimeter framing, ring beams and cross-bracing. It is not capped by a flat roof full of HVAC equipment. The HVAC and lightweight pavilion belong on the low wing roofs.
- The current magenta logo consists of broad rounded forms and a descending 9. Letter geometry and spacing were widened relative to the narrowed cylinder.

The study does **not** claim millimetric accuracy, a surveyed footprint, hidden interior reconstruction, or a current 2026 as-built survey. Campaign artwork remains original and different from the photographed advertisement. Visible architecture is now grounded in close-up photo evidence; the back elevation, exact member sizes, compressed dimensions and some equipment placement remain interpreted.
